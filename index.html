FAQ

Common questions

What is SBC?

SBC is the Senior Blockchain Company token on TON. Its total supply is 256 million tokens.

How do I buy SBC?

Connect your TON wallet and select Buy SBC. Enter a USDT amount, check the quote and price impact, then confirm in your wallet.

How do I sell SBC for USDT?

Select Sell SBC, enter an amount, check the USDT quote and confirm in your wallet.

How is the SBC price calculated?

The base price is the USDT reserve divided by the SBC reserve. Quotes use a constant-product formula. Larger trades have more price impact.

Which wallet do I need?

Use a TON Connect wallet such as Tonkeeper or MyTonWallet. Keep some TON for network fees.

Is SBC Exchange custodial?

You keep your private keys and sign each transfer in your wallet. Buy and sell payments go to the treasury wallet.

What is the official SBC contract address?

SBC contract: EQBIQe_KkVxaJmga7LVgwvB8lcXbbfsqdziGgDXfD-zW4KU9. Verify it before trading.
